# Aryan Rastogi

Consultant at **Boston Consulting Group**, previously Manager IT at **Tata Capital**. MBA from **IIM Ahmedabad**, B.Tech in Electrical Engineering from **IIT Indore**.

Experience in machine learning and NLP, with published research on stress detection from social media text, adversarial defenses for text classifiers and brain-age prediction from MRI scans. Has also built question-answering systems, websites and blockchain systems.

- **Smart India Hackathon 2022 and Inter IIT Tech Meet 10.0:** [SAATHI](https://github.com/aryan-r22/SAATHI_BAND). An IoT wristband and app that helps people with anxiety disorders track and manage their mental health.
